A Granola alternative without the cloud

Granola is lovely. No bot joins your call, and it writes your notes for you. It does that in the cloud, and its paid plan is a subscription.

I wanted fast, simple and robust transcription for my calls that runs on my Mac without sending data to the cloud. That became Dictate: it transcribes your online calls on your Mac, and no bot joins. It doesn't write the notes itself. Your own agent does that, from the transcript.

How it works

  1. Start a call in Zoom, Teams, Slack, a browser tab such as Meet, or another call app. Dictate asks before it records.
  2. It records your microphone and the call's audio separately, so every line is labelled Me or Call.
  3. The call is kept in History on your Mac, searchable.
  4. Ask your agent for the notes, for example "Summarize my call with Alex and draft a follow-up email." Claude Code or any MCP client can read your calls.

Your agent reads the call through a server on your Mac, and where it sends it from there depends on that agent.
