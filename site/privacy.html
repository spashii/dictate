Privacy

Short version: Dictate needs no sign-up. If you signed up earlier, I keep your email, and you can ask me to delete it at any time.

What I store if you signed up

  • Your email address.
  • When you signed up, and which wording of the form you saw.
  • Where you came from, if the link you followed said so (for example a campaign name).
  • Whether a download link was sent to you, whether you opened it, and whether the download started.

I do not store your IP address. To stop spam, I keep a salted, one-way hash of it for about two hours, which cannot be turned back into your address.

What I use it for

To email you occasional beta news. Nothing else, and I do not sell or share your address.

Who handles it

The controller of your data is Sameer, the developer of Dictate. Earlier signups are stored in a database on Cloudflare. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages.

This site counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets no cookies and does not track you across sites.

Deleting your data

Reply to any email from me and ask to be removed. I delete your email and signup record. You can also ask for a copy of what I hold, or for a correction.

Contact

Reply to any email I sent you.

How long I keep it

Until you ask me to delete it.

The app itself

Your audio and transcripts stay on your Mac. The app goes online only to fetch its speech models and to check for updates. It reaches three hosts:

  • huggingface.co, for the speech and voice activity models.
  • apps.firstversion.in, for update checks.
  • dictate.firstversion.in, for update downloads.

The update server counts checks by app, version and country and never stores your IP address. If you connect an agent, it can read your transcripts, and where it sends them depends on that agent.
