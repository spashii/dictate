Privacy

Short version: we keep your email to send you Dictate, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

What we store when you sign up

  • Your email address.
  • When you signed up, and which wording of the form you saw.
  • Where you came from, if the link you followed said so (for example a campaign name).
  • Whether your download link was sent, and whether you opened it.

We do not store your IP address. To stop spam, we keep a salted, one-way hash of it for about two hours, which cannot be turned back into your address.

What we use it for

To email you the download link and occasional beta news. Nothing else, and we do not sell or share your address.

Who handles it

The signup form and its database run on Cloudflare, and the spam check is Cloudflare Turnstile. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets no cookies and does not follow you across sites. We send each email by hand.

Deleting your data

Reply to any email from us and ask to be removed. We delete your email and signup record. You can also ask for a copy of what we hold, or for a correction.

How long we keep it

Until the beta ends or you ask us to delete it, whichever comes first.

The app itself

Your audio stays on your Mac. The app goes online only to fetch its speech model and to check for updates.
