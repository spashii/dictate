# Settings

Settings is in the sidebar of Dictate's window, under History. Cmd+, from the menu bar menu opens it on Setup. Setup shows a badge until its steps are done.

- **Setup:** allow the microphone and Accessibility, download the speech model, then Open at login and Keep history (forever, or 30 days).
- **Shortcuts:** the dictation shortcut, the call shortcut, and whether Dictate asks to record calls (on).
- **Microphones:** the default microphone for dictation and for online calls, and whether Blend Mic is installed. If the chosen microphone isn't connected, the default one records and Dictate says so.
- **Model:** Large v3 turbo (recommended) or Large v3 (3.1 GB, for languages turbo does worse), which can't change during an online call. The language is always detected. Key terms and the ones Dictate suggests, the retry switch with its numbers, and the fix step: a switch (off) and the Ollama model. The key terms go to Whisper whether or not the fix switch is on.
- **Agents:** the switch, the host and port the server listens on, the token, the setup to paste into an agent app, and a switch for each tool.
- **About:** the version, Check for Updates, the automatic switch, and the third-party notices.

Dictate asks on its second launch whether to check for updates automatically. Updates are signed, and Dictate installs only ones signed with its own key.
